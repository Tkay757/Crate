Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Crate is built on privacy. Because our service utilizes WebRTC technology to facilitate direct peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers, we do not upload, store, or view any files you share through our platform. The data is transmitted directly between the sender and the receiver.

2. Data Automatically Collected

To provide and improve our service, we may collect standard connection information automatically, including IP addresses strictly required for STUN/TURN server matchmaking, and basic analytics data.

3. Third-Party Advertising (AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies like Google to serve ads. These companies may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

4. Security

All file transfers are encrypted end-to-end via the WebRTC protocol using DTLS (Datagram Transport Layer Security).
