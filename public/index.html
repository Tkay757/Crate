What is Crate File Sharing Tool?

Crate is a state-of-the-art online file sharing tool designed for absolute speed, privacy, and simplicity. Unlike traditional cloud storage services that upload your personal data to a remote central server, Crate utilizes advanced WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication) technology to create a direct peer-to-peer (P2P) data channel between your devices. This means your files are streamed directly from the sender to the receiver, offering unparalleled transfer speeds—especially on local Wi-Fi networks—while ensuring your data remains completely private and untouched by third parties.

How Does P2P File Sharing Work?

When you select files to send, our tool generates a secure, temporary 6-digit room PIN. The receiver simply enters this PIN on their device to establish a direct cryptographic connection. Because the file transfer completely bypasses third-party hosting servers, there are no artificial file size limits and no waiting for tedious uploads to finish before downloads can begin.

Key Features

  • No File Limits: Share massive 4K videos, heavy zip archives, or thousands of documents effortlessly. Buffering is only limited by your browser's RAM.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files stored on your servers?

No. Crate is a peer-to-peer (P2P) file sharing tool. The server only acts as a matchmaker to connect the two devices using a 6-digit PIN. Once connected, the file is streamed directly from the sender's device to the receiver's device.

Is there a limit to how many files I can send?

There are absolutely no artificial limits. You can send as many files as you want, and they can be as large as you want. The only limitation is the available RAM and storage on your local device.

Does Crate work over the internet or just local Wi-Fi?

Both! Crate uses WebRTC which will automatically find the fastest route. If you are on the same Wi-Fi network, it will transfer locally at extremely high speeds. If you are on different networks, it will securely transfer over the internet.
